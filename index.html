VR & DESKTOP CONTROLS

🎮 META QUEST 3S (VR)

  • Laser Pointer: Right / Left Aim
  • Select / Click: Trigger (Index)
  • 3D Control Panel: Floating in front
  • Natural 6DoF: Walk & Look around
  • Recenter Panel: Panel button or Grip

⌨️ DESKTOP SHORTCUTS

  • Look / Rotate: Mouse Click+Drag
  • Movement: W / A / S / D keys
  • Themes 1-5: Keys 1 - 5
  • Lasers / Particles: L / P
  • Crowd / Artist: C / A
  • Backdrop Mode: B key
  • Pulse Flash: Spacebar
  • Toggle Synth Beat: M key
  • Toggle HUD: H key
WebXR Tip: To enter VR on Quest 3S, click the VR goggles icon at the bottom-right corner of the browser window.
